Maintenance Checklist

Check the Water Conditions

The first step is to text the water parameters with a test kit like API master test kit to see how the water parameters are holding up. If you test with any ammonia, nitrates, or nitrites then its time to do some tank maintenace.

Change Out the Dirty Water

If you find that you need to change the water just drain about 20% to 30% of the tanks water and fill it back up with water. Make sure when you are draining the tank you don't suck up any of your fish.

Put Any Additives For the Fish Health

Next you want to make sure you add any additives that will help to keep your fish healthy. I like to use some fertilizers as well for my aquatic plants health.

Things to be Aware of

When you are changing out the water you will be replacing the water with chlorinated water so make sure to get a declorinator so that the Fish don't become sick.
